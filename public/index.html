DNA Gym AI

Train smarter. Be a stronger you.

DNA Gym AI is a workout planning and training companion designed to help you build workouts, track training, monitor progress and make informed progression decisions.

App store availability coming soon.

In the app

AI-assisted workout planning

Generate a program on your device from the exercise library and your answers. Programs labelled AI are fitness guidance assembled on the device.

Custom workouts

Build a custom session and choose the exercises you want to train.

Live workout tracking

Log sets, reps and weight while you train.

Exercise guidance

Open exercise instructions and demonstration media from the product catalogue.

Progressive overload guidance

Review progression suggestions as a guide, then choose a load you can control.

Workout history

Look back at the strength sessions you have logged.

Cardio tracking

Record cardio alongside strength training.

Progress and body measurements

Enter measurements such as weight, height and body fat, and review them over time.

Apple Health

On iPhone, you can connect Apple Health and import selected measurements and workouts.

Health Connect

On Android, you can connect Health Connect and import selected measurements and exercise sessions.
