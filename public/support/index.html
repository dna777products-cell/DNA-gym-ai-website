DNA Gym AI Support

Need help with DNA Gym AI?

Email is the way to reach support. Include what you were trying to do, whether you are on iPhone or Android, and the email address on the account. Do not send your password.

Email

support@dnagymai.com

What we can help with

  • Account and sign-in help
  • Password reset
  • Invitation and access help
  • Workout questions
  • Apple Health or Health Connect connection issues
  • Technical problems
  • Privacy and account deletion enquiries

Delete your account

You can delete your DNA Gym AI account from inside the app. Open Profile, go to the signed-in Account section, and choose Delete account. The app asks you to confirm before the account is removed.

What deletion removes is described in the Privacy Policy and on the Delete account page. If you cannot sign in to delete the account yourself, email support@dnagymai.com.
